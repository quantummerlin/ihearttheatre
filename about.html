ℹ️ About

We ♥ Theatre

iHeartTheatre is a free community theatre hub — celebrating the shows, people and companies that make community theatre extraordinary.

Our Manifesto

Community theatre is where the magic lives. It's where teachers, nurses, accountants, and teenagers transform themselves every night into heroes, villains, queens and clowns — for the pure love of it. iHeartTheatre exists to celebrate that. To shine a light on every company putting in the work, every performer taking their first terrifying steps on stage, and every show that deserves a bigger audience.

We are independent, free and always will be. No paywalls, no accounts required — just a love of theatre, freely shared.
