## Contact Strategy Notes

1. **Email first** — Use the outreach email template. Most companies list a general email on their website.
2. **Facebook DMs** — Many community companies are most active on Facebook. Use the quick DM version.
3. **TryBooking** — Some companies ONLY post auditions on TryBooking. Search `trybooking.com` + company name.
4. **AusStage** — Check [ausstage.edu.au](https://www.ausstage.edu.au) for company contact details.
5. **Star Now / Showcast** — Some companies also post on casting platforms; search to find their profiles.
6. **Personal intros** — If you know anyone in a company, a personal intro converts much better than cold email.
