## Email Body

Hi [Name / Team],

I'm building **iHeartTheatre** — a free community resource connecting musical theatre performers with audition opportunities across Melbourne and Victoria.

We've just launched our **Audition Noticeboard**, and I'd love to feature **[Company Name]**'s upcoming auditions.

**All we need is a link** to your audition announcement (website, Facebook post, TryBooking — whatever you use). We'll handle the rest and create a listing with all the key details.

It's completely free — for companies and performers. We just want to make it easier for talented people to find great shows.

**To submit:** Just reply to this email with a link, or send it to **auditions@ihearttheatre.com.au** any time.

We'd also love to list you as an ongoing partner so performers can always find your latest opportunities.

Thanks for everything you do for Melbourne theatre! 🎭

Cheers,
[Your Name]
iHeartTheatre
