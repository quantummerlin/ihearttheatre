Lead

Roxie Hart

from Chicago — John Kander
soprano pop beltD4-Bb5Belt required (G4-Bb5)Age 20–38
Roxie is the ultimate theatrical narcissist — a wannabe showgirl who accidentally becomes a celebrity murderess and manipulates everyone around her to stay famous. She's funny, ruthless, delusional, and, in the right hands, oddly endearing. The role demands a triple threat performer who can make Roxie's complete moral bankruptcy genuinely charming.
Jazz-adjacent pop belt. Roxie Me is an introspective waltz. Funny Honey is breathlessly naive and comedic. Mr. Cellophane's counterpart in the finale requires pure pop belt power. The style should feel like a would-be performer — slightly overdone, completely calculated.

What Directors Love to See
  • Fosse technique — the isolations, the style, the precision — this is the core requirement
  • Jazz vocal sensibility, not classical technique
  • The dark comedy — Roxie must be funny about being terrible
  • Acting intelligence — Roxie's manipulation is always calculated
Audition Tips
  • Take a Fosse-style jazz class before auditioning — if you don't have the technique, this isn't the role
  • Show the comedy first — directors need to see Roxie's delusional charm
  • Jazz standard audition pieces work well for demonstrating vocal style
Common Mistakes to Avoid
  • Playing Roxie as purely cynical — her delusion is what makes her funny and oddly sweet
  • Using operatic or theatre belt technique instead of jazz pop belt
