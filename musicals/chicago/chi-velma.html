Velma Kelly

from Chicago — John Kander
mezzo beltG3-G5Belt required (Eb5-G5)Age 22–42
Velma is the real deal — a genuine performer with actual talent who opened the show with one of the best opening numbers in all of musical theatre. Her problem is that Roxie stole her spotlight and she has to figure out what to do about it. She's smarter than Roxie, more controlled, and in many ways more dangerous.
Powerhouse mezzo belt with genuine jazz and blues swagger. 'All That Jazz' requires immediate, explosive authority. The voice should sound like someone who has performed in Chicago supper clubs for years — polished, powerful, dangerous.

What Directors Love to See
  • 'All That Jazz' — the opening requires explosive authority from the first note
  • Fosse technique at the highest level — Velma is the professional in the room
  • Strategic intelligence — Velma is always running the play
  • The ability to cede focus to Roxie while retaining her dominant energy
Audition Tips
  • Show the authority first — 'All That Jazz' should feel like a done deal
  • Velma and Roxie are equals who need each other — show you can play that dynamic
Common Mistakes to Avoid
  • Playing Velma as overly aggressive — her threat comes from total control, not aggression
