Lead

Elsa

from Frozen — Kristen Anderson-Lopez & Robert Lopez
soprano mezzo beltBb3-C6Belt required (F5-C6)Age 18–35
The Snow Queen. Isolated, controlled, and carrying an enormous secret. Elsa is one of the great contemporary female leads: a woman whose power is also her wound. She must be regal, vulnerable, and explosive in equal measure.
Requires a powerful contemporary Broadway belt that can sound both icy and deeply emotional. Let It Go demands a full belt to a top C. The voice should feel like power and pain simultaneously.

What Directors Love to See
  • Let It Go - the belt must be secure and powerful, not reached for
  • The controlled exterior that contains enormous emotion
  • The full dramatic range from Act 1 isolation to Act 2 acceptance
  • Vocal stamina - the show is vocally punishing
Audition Tips
  • Let It Go is the natural audition piece but Monster will distinguish you
  • Show the restraint before the release - the explosion only means something if the control was real
  • Work with a vocal coach specialising in contemporary Broadway belt
Common Mistakes to Avoid
  • Playing Elsa as cold - she is terrified, not cold
  • Reaching for Let It Go rather than owning it
