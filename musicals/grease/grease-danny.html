Lead

Danny Zuko

from Grease — Jim Jacobs & Warren Casey
rock baritoneBb2-F4Belt required (D4-F4)Age 17–28
Danny is the coolest guy at Rydell — and beneath the leather jacket, a genuinely sweet boy who's fallen in love and doesn't know how to reconcile that with his image. The role requires physical charisma, genuine rock and roll energy, and the ability to make Danny's insecurity charming rather than irritating.
Rock baritone with 50s pop swagger. Danny You're Lousy requires the full range of emotional expression from soft vulnerability to rock swagger. Summer Nights should feel effortless and fun.

What Directors Love to See
  • Natural physical charisma and 50s swagger
  • Comedy — Danny's gang scenes should be genuinely funny
  • The vulnerability that breaks through the cool exterior in the romantic scenes
  • Strong dance ability, particularly the Hand Jive
Audition Tips
  • Sandy must be clearly the most important thing in his life even when he's pretending otherwise
  • The comedy of trying-to-be-cool-while-being-besotted is most of the role — lean into it
Common Mistakes to Avoid
  • Playing Danny as genuinely cool rather than desperately trying to be cool
  • Missing the sweetness under the swagger
