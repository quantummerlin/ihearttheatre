Lead

Sandy Olsson

from Grease — Jim Jacobs & Warren Casey
soprano to belt mezzoC4-Bb5Belt required (G4-Bb5)Age 17–28
Sandy starts the show as the sweetest, most wholesome character on stage — and ends it as the most transformed. The arc from wholesome Australian ingenue to confident woman (in that iconic black outfit) is one of the great character journeys in popular theatre. Vocally, Sandy spans from pure soprano to a driving belt across the show.
Sandy's early songs (Hopelessly Devoted) sit in a pure lyric soprano range. You're the One That I Want requires a pop belt that matches Danny's energy. The contrast between Act 1 sweetness and Act 2 confidence is the whole vocal arc.

What Directors Love to See
  • 'Hopelessly Devoted' shows the pure soprano quality and emotional vulnerability
  • Physical transformation ability — the Act 2 Sandy must feel genuinely different
  • Chemistry with Danny in the audition room
Audition Tips
  • Show both aspects — the innocent early Sandy AND the confident finale Sandy if possible
  • Hopelessly Devoted is a beautiful audition piece; don't oversell the emotion — let the song do the work
  • The physical transformation should feel like confidence, not just a costume — work on that quality
Common Mistakes to Avoid
  • Playing Sandy as empty-headed — she's sweet AND smart
  • Making the Act 2 transformation too abrupt — it should feel like she found herself, not just changed for a boy
