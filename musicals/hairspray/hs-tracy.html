Lead

Tracy Turnblad

from Hairspray — Marc Shaiman
mezzo beltBb3-G5Belt required (D5-G5)Age 14–25
Tracy is one of musical theatre's great heroines — not because she's superhuman, but because her joy is completely genuine and her goodness is never smug. She dances not because she's the best dancer but because dancing is pure happiness. Tracy doesn't see colour or size — she just sees people she wants to include. She's one of the most infectious leading characters in contemporary musical theatre.
Full, warm mezzo belt in the Motown/soul tradition. 'Good Morning Baltimore' should feel effortlessly joyful — the belt is big but the character carries it lightly. 'I Know Where I've Been' (if doubled with Maybelle) is a different quality entirely.

Common casting: Belt mezzos with genuine stage warmth, strong dance ability, and natural likability. Tracy is frequently cast against type in terms of body — the role celebrates real diversity.

What Directors Love to See
  • The joy must be completely genuine — Tracy's energy is the show's entire engine
  • 'Good Morning Baltimore' — the belt must be big but the character must be bigger
  • Dance ability in the 60s Motown style — Tracy's dancing IS the premise
Audition Tips
  • Good Morning Baltimore is the definitive audition piece — show the joy first and foremost
  • Don't play Tracy as a plucky underdog — she genuinely doesn't see herself as disadvantaged
  • The 60s dance vocabulary should feel natural, not studied
Common Mistakes to Avoid
  • Playing Tracy as sweet — she's joyful, which is different and more infectious
  • Underestimating the dance requirements — Tracy's success depends on her dancing
