Éponine

from Les Misérables — Claude-Michel Schönberg
mezzo-soprano beltG3-F5Belt required (B4-F5)Age 16–30
Éponine is the fan favourite of Les Mis — a street girl who loves Marius with everything she has while he pursues another. 'On My Own' is one of the most performed songs in musical theatre auditions worldwide. Her sacrifice on the barricade is one of the show's most affecting moments.
Street-smart mezzo with a raw, real quality. 'On My Own' should sound like someone telling the truth, not performing a ballad. The key change must feel like emotional escalation, not just volume.

What Directors Love to See
  • 'On My Own' — emotional truth above all technique. Distinguish your interpretation
  • The street-smart physicality that reveals a tender interior
  • The love for Marius must be completely real — Éponine's sacrifice carries the whole Act 2 barricade
Audition Tips
  • 'On My Own' is the natural audition but because it's SO common, a distinctive personal interpretation is crucial
  • Show the street kid before you show the romantic — the contrast IS the character
  • A Little Fall of Rain requires genuine emotional intimacy — the death scene is unguarded
Common Mistakes to Avoid
  • Playing the performance of the song rather than the truth of it
  • Making Éponine too polished — she should feel raw and real
  • Missing the anger underneath the love — Éponine knows she will never have what she wants
