Lead

Matilda Wormwood

from Matilda The Musical — Tim Minchin
child soprano/beltA3-E5Belt required (C4-D5)Age 8–13
A brilliant, book-loving child stuck with dreadful parents and a monstrous headmistress. She's brave, compassionate, fiercely intelligent, and discovers she has telekinetic powers. Must carry the entire show with both emotional depth and comedic timing. One of the most demanding child roles in musical theatre.
Must handle Tim Minchin's rapid-fire, word-heavy lyrics with absolute clarity. Needs genuine belt power for 'Naughty' and 'Revolting Children' but also pure, sweet tone for 'Quiet'. British accent required. The vocal demands are extraordinary for a child performer.

One of the greatest child roles ever written. Demands everything — vocal power, acting depth, physical stamina, diction, emotional range. Career-launching for young performers. Typically triple-cast due to demands.
Common casting: Exceptional child performer with natural intelligence, strong voice, and work ethic. Must genuinely seem like the smartest person in the room.

Acting

Advanced

Must be genuinely convincing as a genius child — not precocious but authentically brilliant. Needs to hold audience attention against scene-stealing adult characters. The storytelling sequences require real narrative skill. Emotional arc from lonely to empowered must be earned.

What Directors Love to See
  • natural intelligence — not performed
  • strong clear voice with belt
  • perfect diction
  • genuine emotional depth
  • stamina and focus
  • British accent ability
Audition Tips
  • Matilda is not a cute kid — she's a fierce, brilliant mind in a small body. Read voraciously before auditioning. Understand what it means to be the smartest person in a room full of adults. Practice Tim Minchin's lyrics until they feel like natural speech.
Common Mistakes to Avoid
  • being cute instead of smart
  • poor diction on fast songs
  • not enough emotional range
  • running out of energy
