Lead

Jack Kelly

from Newsies The Musical — Alan Menken
tenor/baritoneBb2-A4Belt required (E4-A4)Age 16–28
The charismatic, dreaming leader of the Manhattan newsies. An orphan with a burning desire to escape to Santa Fe, Jack becomes the unexpected face of the strike. He's passionate, impulsive, occasionally conflicted — but always magnetic. The show's emotional and physical centre. One of the great leading male roles in contemporary musical theatre.
Strong, driving rock-influenced tenor belt. Must handle both intimate ballad moments ('Santa Fe' as internal monologue) and massive anthemic belt ('Seize the Day', 'King of New York'). Clarity on fast ensemble passages essential. Raw, honest vocal quality — not polished, but powerful.

Jack Kelly is one of the defining leading male roles of the modern era. Demands complete performer integration — singing, dancing, acting, physical theatre, leading an ensemble. A career-defining role for the right performer.
Common casting: A triple threat male performer who leads by presence, not by volume. Must be physically capable (acrobatics a strong advantage) and have genuine charisma that reads from the back row.

Dance

Advanced

Jack is in almost every major dance number. Must have strong jazz and acrobatic foundation. 'Seize the Day' and 'King of New York' are massive full-company numbers demanding constant high energy. Partnering required for Katherine scenes.

Advanced

Must be genuinely magnetic — the ensemble follows Jack, so the audience must believe they would too. The Santa Fe dream sequence requires real solo acting. Romance with Katherine must feel earned, not performative.

Jack needs a tenor belt to A4 with rock grit and emotional authenticity. He is not a polished vocalist — he's raw, honest, powerful. Acrobatic ability is as important as voice at auditions. Bring both.
Avoid: Over-polished classical tenor quality — Jack should sound like the streets, not a conservatory
What Directors Love to See
  • physical power and acrobatic ability
  • strong tenor belt to A4
  • natural charisma and leadership presence
  • genuine emotional depth in ballad moments
  • can hold a room without trying
Audition Tips
  • Jack is a dreamer who hides vulnerability behind bravado. Find the boy inside the leader. 'Santa Fe' should feel like he's talking to himself — the audience is eavesdropping. Your acrobatic ability will be crucial — come prepared to move.
Common Mistakes to Avoid
  • generic hero energy without specificity
  • prioritising tricks over character
  • treating Santa Fe as a showcase number instead of a private moment
  • not connecting with ensemble members during group numbers
