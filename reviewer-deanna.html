About Deanna

Deanna Amato writes about theatre because she loves it. She started going because of Penelope, and she never stopped. There is beauty in every show, in the work, the commitment, the people on stage, and that's what she writes about. Not what went wrong. What was wonderful.


She covers everything from big professional seasons to community halls and school stages across Melbourne and Victoria. Broadway or a local hall, she walks in wanting the show to be great. And she writes about it like it was.
