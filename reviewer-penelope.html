Meet Penelope

Penelope Quinn's love of theatre goes back to her Nonna, who passed it down through the family one show at a time. After Nonna passed away, a chance visit to Theatrical's The Wizard of Oz at the National Theatre changed everything. It was opening night, floods, fire escape entrance, the lot. Penelope came as Dorothy (pigtails, basket, fluffy dog) and stole hearts. Her clip ended up in Theatrical's highlights reel, the cast wanted to meet her, and a backstage visit at interval sealed the deal. A cast member whispered that there was a new show coming - The Sound of Music - and that she should audition.


Penelope was still very young when she was cast as Gretl in The Sound of Music by Theatrical. Three months of rehearsals, every show of the season, selling programs, getting a proper look at how a production works from the inside. Since then she's trained at Chris Horsey's XLR8 school, performed in Shrek Jr., Dare to Dream, Aristocats and Theatrical Symphony, and been cast by Williamstown Theatre Company in Newsies.


As a reviewer, Penelope gives an honest kid's take on everything she sees, from school halls to professional seasons. She's reviewed for thetheatre.au, reported from the front row for Theatrical's Three Little Pigs, been in Sunshine's Star Weekly twice, in The Age with her Sound of Music castmates, and done a weather segment on Channel 10.
