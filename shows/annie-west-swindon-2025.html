About the Show

Annie is one of the most beloved family musicals of all time, following the indomitable spirit of a plucky red-headed orphan growing up in 1930s New York City. When the fabulously wealthy Oliver Warbucks invites an orphan to spend Christmas at his mansion, little Annie seizes her chance — capturing the billionaire's heart and launching a desperate search for her long-lost parents.

Packed with unforgettable songs including "Tomorrow", "It's the Hard-Knock Life", "Easy Street", and "NYC", this musical brims with heart, humour, and the unshakeable belief that the sun will always come out tomorrow — no matter how tough things get.

The Production

West Swindon Singers brings a talented cast of young and adult performers together for a production that perfectly balances nostalgia with fresh energy, making this run at the Wyvern Theatre a must-see for families and musical theatre fans alike across the whole Swindon area.
