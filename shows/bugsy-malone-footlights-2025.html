About the Show

It's 1929 New York, and two rival gangs are battling for control of the city: the smooth-talking Fat Sam Stacetto and the menacing Dandy Dan, whose gang has got hold of a terrifying new weapon — the splurge gun. Into this mob-ruled world wanders Bugsy Malone, a charming all-round good guy who just can't seem to stay out of trouble, especially when the dazzling Blousey Brown arrives in town hoping to make it big in Hollywood.

Alan Parker's genius conceit is that every role — every gangster, every chorus girl, every speakeasy crooner — is played entirely by children, armed with splurge guns that fire custard pies instead of bullets. The result is a gloriously anarchic, thoroughly loveable show with an irresistible score that includes "Fat Sam's Grand Slam", "My Name Is Tallulah" and the unforgettable "You Give a Little Love".

The Production

Footlights Youth Theatre has been nurturing young performers in the Swindon area for over two decades, and this production of Bugsy Malone showcases the remarkable talent coming through its training programme. Expect formidable energy, impeccable comic timing, and enough custard pies to keep the whole family entertained.
