About the Show

Set in 1930s Berlin as the Nazi shadow falls across Germany, Cabaret plunges the audience into the seductive, decadent world of the Kit Kat Club. American writer Cliff Bradshaw arrives in Berlin and falls for the irrepressible English showgirl Sally Bowles, while the Emcee — the Club's sardonic, glittering host — comments on the gathering darkness with unsettling brilliance. With a score by John Kander and lyrics by Fred Ebb, including iconic numbers such as "Willkommen", "Maybe This Time", and the defiant title song, Cabaret is one of the most dazzling and devastating musicals ever written.

Beneath the glitter and spectacle lies a profoundly human story about love, denial, and the danger of looking away from the world around you. Cabaret's electrifying combination of cabaret performance and dramatic narrative makes it a show that haunts audiences long after the curtain falls.

The Production

Swindon Musical Theatre brings its considerable talent and theatrical ambition to the Wyvern Theatre for this eagerly anticipated autumn production. Known for staging bold, large-scale musicals with professional polish, the company promises a Cabaret that honours the show's dark glamour in full — a must-see event for the Swindon arts calendar.
