About the Show

Set in the razzle-dazzle world of 1920s Jazz-Age Chicago, this Kander and Ebb masterpiece follows Roxie Hart — a wannabe vaudeville star who murders her lover and talks her way into notoriety with the help of the slickest lawyer in town, Billy Flynn. Alongside fellow jail-bird Velma Kelly, Roxie navigates a corrupt justice system where celebrity is the only currency that matters and murder is merely a matter of press management.

Featuring some of the most electric numbers in the musical theatre canon — "All That Jazz", "Cell Block Tango", "Roxie", and "Razzle Dazzle" — Chicago is a darkly comic, breathtakingly choreographed satire on fame, justice, and the American dream that feels as sharp today as it did at its Broadway debut.

The Production

Swindon Operatic Society tackles the Bob Fosse-inspired demands of this iconic show with conviction, fielding a powerhouse lead cast and a chorus that delivers the sultry, hard-edged choreography with tremendous style. Last chance to catch this production at the Wyvern — do not let it close without you.
