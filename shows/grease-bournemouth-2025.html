About the Show

Set in the sun-drenched summer of 1958, Grease follows the whirlwind romance between Danny Zuko, the cool leader of the T-Birds, and Sandy Olsson, the sweet new girl at Rydell High. When their summer fling unexpectedly collides with high school reality, both must decide how far they'll go — and who they really want to be.

Packed with iconic songs including "Summer Nights", "Greased Lightnin'", "Hopelessly Devoted to You" and "You're the One That I Want", Grease is a joyous celebration of teenage rebellion, first love, and the irresistible power of rock 'n' roll. It remains one of the best-loved musicals of all time for very good reason.

The Production

Bournemouth Musical Theatre brings their trademark high-energy staging to the spectacular Lighthouse, Poole — the South West's largest arts centre — promising a production bursting with authentic 1950s style, electrifying choreography, and that infectious feel-good factor that only Grease can deliver. Don't miss your chance to catch this beloved classic live on stage this May.
