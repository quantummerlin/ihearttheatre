About the Show

Set among the colourful gamblers, showgirls and small-time hustlers of 1950s New York, Guys and Dolls is the ultimate Broadway classic. High-roller Sky Masterson accepts a bet from fellow gambler Nathan Detroit: take a doll — any doll — to Havana for dinner. The catch? Sky chooses the strait-laced missionary Sarah Brown, setting off a comedy of mixed motives, clashing worlds, and hearts that refuse to follow the odds.

Frank Loesser's glorious score brims with wit and warmth, from the brassy bravado of "Sit Down, You're Rockin' the Boat" and "Luck Be a Lady" to the tender romance of "I've Never Been in Love Before" and "If I Were a Bell". Guys and Dolls is pure theatrical joy — funny, stylish, and completely irresistible.

The Production

The Dorset Operatic Society is renowned throughout the region for their vibrant, polished productions, and this staging of Guys and Dolls at the Lighthouse, Poole is expected to be one of their finest hours. With a cast of outstanding singers and dancers and a flair for the glamour of the Broadway golden age, expect sequins, snap-brim hats, and showstopping entertainment from first curtain to last.
