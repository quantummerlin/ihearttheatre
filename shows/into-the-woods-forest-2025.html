About the Show

Stephen Sondheim and James Lapine's masterpiece weaves together the stories of Cinderella, Little Red Riding Hood, Jack and the Beanstalk, and Rapunzel alongside a childless Baker and his Wife who venture into the enchanted forest to break a witch's curse. In the first act, every fairy tale hero pursues their deepest wish — "I wish...", "I want...", "Some day my prince will come..." — and each dream comes true. But Act Two reveals the devastating consequences that follow when wishes are granted without care.

With Sondheim's razor-sharp score — featuring "Children Will Listen", "No One Is Alone", "Agony", and "The Last Midnight" — Into the Woods stands as one of the most sophisticated and emotionally profound musicals ever written, asking what it truly means to be careful what you wish for.

The Production

Forest Youth Theatre brings a remarkable young ensemble to the dramatic clifftop stage of the legendary Minack Theatre, where the natural Cornish setting lends the enchanted forest scenes a genuinely otherworldly atmosphere. Staging Sondheim outdoors with the Atlantic as backdrop is an extraordinary theatrical undertaking — and not to be missed.
