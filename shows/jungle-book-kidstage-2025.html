About the Show

Inspired by Rudyard Kipling's timeless tales, The Jungle Book follows young Mowgli — a boy raised by wolves deep in the Indian jungle — as he navigates a world of extraordinary animal friends and fearsome enemies. When the fearsome tiger Shere Khan returns to the jungle, Mowgli must undertake an epic journey with the carefree bear Baloo and the wise panther Bagheera, discovering where he truly belongs along the way.

Packed with exhilarating song and dance, vivid characters, and the kind of adventure that sparks the imagination, this musical adaptation brings Kipling's jungle to life in spectacular style. The show is a joyful celebration of friendship, identity, and the courage to face the unknown — perfect for audiences young and old.

The Production

Kidstage Productions stages this magical adventure at the iconic cliff-top Minack Theatre in Cornwall, one of the most breathtaking outdoor venues in the world. The Minack's natural stone amphitheatre, perched above the Atlantic, lends itself perfectly to the wild grandeur of Kipling's jungle, and Kidstage's talented young cast promise a production as unforgettable as the setting itself.
