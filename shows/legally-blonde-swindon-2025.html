About the Show

Elle Woods has it all: she's the president of her sorority, a fashion design major, and the most popular girl at CULA. But when her boyfriend Warner dumps her before heading to Harvard Law School — telling her she's not "serious" enough — Elle decides to follow him and prove everyone wrong. Armed with her signature pink outfits, a loyal chihuahua named Bruiser, and a fierce belief in herself, she sets out to conquer the most prestigious law school in America.

Packed with infectious pop-rock anthems, high-energy choreography, and a heart-warming message about staying true to who you are, Legally Blonde The Musical is one of the most joyful shows in the modern musical theatre canon. With songs like "Omigod You Guys", "Bend and Snap" and "So Much Better", it's impossible not to leave with a smile.

The Production

Swindon Operatic Society brings its trademark energy and polished ensemble work to the Wyvern Theatre's main stage for this summer spectacular. Established for over a century, the society has long been one of Wiltshire's most ambitious amateur companies, and this production promises all the pink glitter and girl-power anthems the show deserves.
