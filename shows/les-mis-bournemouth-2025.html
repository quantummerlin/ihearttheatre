About the Show

Victor Hugo's sweeping epic comes to blazing life in this magnificent musical by Claude-Michel Schönberg and Alain Boublil. The story follows Jean Valjean — a man condemned to nineteen years in prison for stealing a loaf of bread — as he breaks parole and reinvents himself, hunted across decades of revolutionary France by the relentless Inspector Javert. Along the way, he adopts the child Cosette and becomes entangled in the doomed Paris uprising of 1832, where young idealists fight and die for a better world.

Featuring some of the most breathtaking music ever written for the stage — "I Dreamed a Dream", "One Day More", "Bring Him Home", "On My Own" and "Do You Hear the People Sing?" — Les Misérables is a towering achievement that asks the deepest questions about justice, mercy, love, and redemption.

The Production

Bournemouth G&S Society takes on one of musical theatre's most ambitious and demanding works, bringing their wealth of choral experience and dramatic power to the grand Pavilion stage. With a cast of dozens and a score requiring exceptional vocal strength across every register, this promises to be a truly memorable and moving production in the heart of Bournemouth.
