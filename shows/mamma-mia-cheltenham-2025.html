About the Show

Mamma Mia! is the irresistible feel-good musical built around the timeless pop hits of ABBA. On the eve of her wedding, young Sophie discovers three letters written by her mother Donna and secretly invites the three men who might be her father — Sam, Harry, and Bill — to her Greek island home, setting off a whirlwind of misunderstandings, revelations, and joyful reunion.

With songs including "Dancing Queen", "Super Trouper", "Take a Chance on Me", and "The Winner Takes It All", this production promises an evening bursting with laughter, romance, and the kind of sheer theatrical exuberance that only great ABBA music can deliver.

The Production

Cheltenham Operatic Society brings its characteristic warmth and professional polish to the Everyman Theatre's beloved stage, pairing an ensemble of accomplished local performers with a high-energy staging that celebrates both the glittering disco era and the enduring power of a great song. Do not miss their final performances this May.
