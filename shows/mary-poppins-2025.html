About the Show

Based on the beloved stories of P.L. Travers and the classic Disney film, Mary Poppins the musical sweeps audiences away to Number 17 Cherry Tree Lane, where the Banks family is in need of a very particular sort of nanny. When Mary Poppins arrives — carpet bag in hand, umbrella aloft — she transforms the lives of young Jane and Michael with a blend of magic, adventure, and a spoonful of wisdom. Alongside her friend and confidant Bert the chimney sweep, Mary leads the children on extraordinary journeys to rooftops, chalk pavement drawings, and beyond.

Featuring some of the most cherished songs in musical theatre — "A Spoonful of Sugar", "Supercalifragilisticexpialidocious", "Feed the Birds", and "Anything Can Happen" — this stage adaptation by Julian Fellowes adds new depth and spectacle to a story that has enchanted generations. It is a production full of warmth, wonder, and spectacular theatrical magic.

The Production

West Swindon Singers close their 2025 season with this feel-good family spectacular at the Wyvern Theatre. With a reputation built on superb choral work and strong ensemble storytelling, the company is perfectly placed to deliver the soaring score and the heart at the centre of this timeless show — the ideal evening out for families and musical theatre fans alike this November.
