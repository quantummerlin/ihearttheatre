About the Show

Based on Roald Dahl's beloved novel and the Tony and Olivier Award-winning musical by Tim Minchin, Matilda Jr. tells the story of an extraordinary little girl who, armed with a vivid imagination and a sharp mind, dares to take a stand against the adults in her life. Neglected by her self-absorbed parents and terrorised by the tyrannical headmistress Miss Trunchbull, young Matilda finds solace in books, friendship, and the kind-hearted Miss Honey.

Full of rousing songs, dazzling wordplay, and irresistible charm, Matilda Jr. celebrates the power of courage and the magic that can happen when children refuse to accept the world as it is. This junior adaptation retains all the wit and heart of the original while giving young performers centre stage in a production perfectly sized for youth casts.

The Production

Junior Theatre Workshop brings boundless energy and fresh talent to this Swindon summer production, staged at the Arts Centre on Devizes Road. Renowned for nurturing young performers across Swindon, the Workshop delivers polished, joyful shows that never fail to delight audiences of all ages — and this Matilda promises to be no exception.
