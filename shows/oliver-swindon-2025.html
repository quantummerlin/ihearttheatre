About the Show

Based on Charles Dickens' beloved novel Oliver Twist, this timeless musical follows young orphan Oliver Twist as he escapes the workhouse and falls in with Fagin's gang of pickpockets on the fog-drenched streets of Victorian London. With nowhere to turn and danger at every corner, Oliver's search for a place to belong becomes a gripping tale of innocence, resilience, and the possibility of kindness in a harsh world.

Lionel Bart's masterpiece is filled with unforgettable songs — "Consider Yourself", "Food, Glorious Food", "As Long as He Needs Me" and "I'd Do Anything" — that have enchanted audiences for over six decades. By turns funny, tender and heartbreaking, Oliver! is the kind of musical that stays with you long after the curtain falls.

The Production

Swindon Musical Theatre is one of the region's most accomplished amateur companies, with a long history of delivering polished, professional-quality productions at the Swindon Arts Centre. Their staging of Oliver! is set to be a landmark event, bringing the gaslit streets of Dickensian London vividly to life with an ensemble cast of exceptional local talent.
