About the Show

Set in the breathtaking Austrian Alps on the eve of the Second World War, The Sound of Music follows Maria, a free-spirited young postulant who leaves the Abbey to become governess to the seven children of the widowed Captain von Trapp. Her warmth, music, and irrepressible spirit transform the household — and capture the Captain's heart. But as Nazi forces tighten their grip on Austria, the family must make a courageous choice about their future.

Rodgers and Hammerstein's final collaboration remains one of the most beloved musicals ever written, filled with timeless songs including "My Favourite Things", "Edelweiss", "Climb Ev'ry Mountain" and the glorious title number. Equal parts romance, family drama, and wartime adventure, it never fails to move audiences to tears — and their feet.

The Production

Cheltenham Operatic Society, one of the Cotswolds' most celebrated amateur companies, brings its signature blend of vocal excellence and theatrical ambition to the Everyman's gorgeous Victorian auditorium. With a large cast of children and adults, this production promises all the sweep and spectacle that Rodgers and Hammerstein's masterpiece demands.
