About the Show

Benjamin Barker, a skilled barber unjustly transported to Australia by the corrupt Judge Turpin who coveted his wife, returns to Victorian London as the vengeful Sweeney Todd. Reunited with the resourceful pie-maker Mrs Lovett — who reveals the tragic fate of his family — Todd opens a new barber's shop above her parlour, plotting cold-blooded revenge while dispatching an ever-growing list of clients whose remains conveniently end up in Mrs Lovett's infamous meat pies.

Stephen Sondheim and Hugh Wheeler's 1979 masterpiece is a towering work of musical theatre: a Grand Guignol tale of obsession, injustice, and industrialised London horror set to one of the most complex and rewarding scores ever written. Dark, thrilling, and laced with pitch-black humour, it challenges its performers and haunts its audiences long after the curtain falls.

The Production

Oxford Stage Company is renowned across Oxfordshire for tackling the most demanding works in the repertoire, and this production of Sondheim's bleak masterpiece is their most ambitious undertaking yet. Directed with a cinematic eye for atmosphere and featuring a cast of exceptional vocal talent, this is a Sweeney Todd that dares to go to the darkest corners of Fleet Street.
